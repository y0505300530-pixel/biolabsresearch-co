Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Overview

LEEDS MARKETING GROUP LTD ("BioLabs Research", "we", "us") operates biofirst.co. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Contact information: name, email, phone, organization — when you submit an inquiry or place an order.
  • Order data: product selections, quantities, shipping address.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages visited — via standard server logs.
  • Communication data: content of messages you send us via contact forms or email.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To receive and respond to order inquiries and, if we accept an order, to arrange fulfilment.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send replies about inquiries, and shipping notices if an order is fulfilled.
  • To maintain records for compliance and regulatory purposes.
  • To improve our website and services.

4. Inquiries and payments

BioLabs Research is an inquiry-based research-use-only (RUO) shop. The website checkout submits an order inquiry (contact details, shipping address, and requested SKUs). We do not collect card numbers, bank details, or cryptocurrency wallets on this site, and we do not use a third-party payment processor on biofirst.co. If we later arrange payment with you off-site, those details are exchanged directly with you — not through a card form on this website.

Inquiry records (name, email, phone, organisation, address, requested items, and notes) may be stored on our servers and in our email inbox so we can reply and, if agreed, fulfil a research order.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Shipping carriers to deliver orders.
  • Regulatory authorities if legally required.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for cart functionality and site operation. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact info@biofirst.co.
