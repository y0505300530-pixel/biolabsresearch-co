Solution dilution calculator

Laboratory dilution math only: enter the solid amount in the vial (mg) and the solvent volume you add (mL) to get concentration in mg/mL. Optionally enter an aliquot volume to see mass in that aliquot (mg).

—
Concentration (mg/mL)
—
Mass in aliquot (mg)

How it works

Concentration (mg/mL) = vial amount (mg) ÷ solvent volume (mL).

Aliquot mass (mg) = concentration (mg/mL) × aliquot volume (mL).

Worked example

10 mg vial + 2 mL solvent → 5 mg/mL. A 0.1 mL aliquot holds 0.5 mg.
