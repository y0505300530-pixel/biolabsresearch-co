Laboratory handling

Reconstitution is a bench step.

Working solution in mg/mL for in-vitro work. Not a dose. Not a body-weight chart.

  1. 01

    Lyophilized cake

    The vial is a dry cake or powder. Record the labeled milligrams before you open it.

  2. 02

    Add solvent

    Introduce a measured volume of the solvent specified in your protocol. Do not invent a volume here.

  3. 03

    Working solution

    Concentration = labeled mg ÷ mL added. Write mg/mL on the tube. That is lab arithmetic, not a regimen.

  4. 04

    Store as the COA says

    Follow the lot sheet for temperature and retest date. Research use only.
