Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Processing Time

Inquiries are processed within 1–2 business days of confirmation. Research compounds require verification of intended use before shipment.

2. Shipping Methods

Choose a method at checkout. These are the only two options — the same ones shown on the inquiry form.

  • USPS Ground Advantage (standard): 3–5 business days within the US. Free.
  • UPS 2nd Day Air (express): 2 business days, no delivery guarantee. $18.99.

Temperature-sensitive lots ship with cold-chain packing on either method. Priority cold-chain packing is an inquiry reward at $375, not a third carrier.

International: DDP to select markets after we confirm the inquiry. Contact us for availability.

3. Shipping Costs

USPS Ground is free. UPS 2nd Day Air is $18.99, added on the inquiry form. Inquiries of $100+ may include a $0 research solvent (BAC water) marked laboratory use only. No reconstitution or human-use instructions.

4. Order Tracking

Tracking information is provided via email once your order ships. You can also contact us at admin@biolabsresearch.co for status updates.

5. Customs & Import

International shipments may be subject to customs inspection. BioLabs Research provides CoA, SDS, and origin documentation to facilitate clearance. Purchasers are responsible for ensuring compliance with their jurisdiction's import regulations for research compounds.

6. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, contact us within 48 hours at admin@biolabsresearch.co with your order number. We will arrange a replacement or refund.
