Shipping methods

Choose a method on the inquiry form. These are the only two options.

  • USPS Ground Advantage (standard): 3–5 business days within the US. Free.
  • UPS 2nd Day Air (express): 2 business days, no delivery guarantee. $18.99.

Temperature-sensitive lots ship with cold-chain packing on either method. Priority cold-chain packing is an inquiry reward at $375 — not a third carrier. International DDP to select markets after we confirm the inquiry.

Costs & extras

USPS Ground is free. UPS 2nd Day Air is $18.99. Inquiries of $100+ may include a $0 research solvent (BAC water) marked laboratory use only. No reconstitution or human-use instructions.

International shipments may face customs inspection. We can provide CoA, SDS, and origin documentation. Purchasers remain responsible for import rules in their jurisdiction.
