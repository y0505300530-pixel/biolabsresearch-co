What differs at the molecular level

Tesamorelin (CAS 218949-48-5) is a longer GHRH-analogue research peptide listed here as lyophilized material at 10 mg and 20 mg strengths. Identity checks should include CAS and any salt/form notes on the COA.

Ipamorelin (CAS 170851-70-4) is a pentapeptide/GHRP-style research reagent listed at 10 mg. Do not confuse this CAS with unrelated small peptides; the Wave-1 SoT locks 170851-70-4 as the storefront identity.

Literature often places these molecules in related but non-identical axis models. For a pre-combined presentation, see the blend PDP rather than assuming vial ratios. Related: ipamorelin vs sermorelin, blend identity blog.

Tesamorelin when the protocol names the GHRH-analogue identity (CAS 218949-48-5).

Ipamorelin when the protocol names CAS 170851-70-4 / ipamorelin hexapeptide identity.
