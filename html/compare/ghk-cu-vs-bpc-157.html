GHK-Cu vs BPC-157: research comparison

GHK-Cu and BPC-157 are both present in the BioLabs Research catalog but represent unrelated chemical identities. GHK-Cu is a copper(II)–tripeptide complex; BPC-157 is a pentadecapeptide. Comparing them is useful for procurement clarity, not because they share a pathway label in marketing copy.

GHK-Cu

GHK-Cu (CAS 89030-95-5) is listed with formula C14H22CuN6O4 and approximate MW ~403.9 Da on site identity strips. The copper complexation is part of the identity — a copper-free GHK listing would be a different reagent. Research literature often places GHK-Cu in extracellular matrix and copper-handling model systems.

See GHK-Cu copper peptide research and GHK-Cu product. GLOW 70 is a separate named blend SKU that should not be confused with neat GHK-Cu.

BPC-157

BPC-157 (CAS 137525-51-0) carries formula C62H98N16O22, MW ~1419.5 Da, and sequence GEPPPGKPADDAGLV on this site. It contains no copper center. Analytical expectations (HPLC, MS) differ from metal–peptide complexes; treat COA methods accordingly.

Overview: BPC-157 research overview · product.

Key differences

  • Metal complex (Cu) vs non-metallated pentadecapeptide.
  • Different CAS, formula, MW, and sequence.
  • Distinct research literature clusters; not drop-in replacements.
  • Both sold as separate catalog vials with their own strength options.

Which for which research context

GHK-Cu when the protocol requires the copper–tripeptide complex (CAS 89030-95-5).

BPC-157 when the protocol requires GEPPPGKPADDAGLV / CAS 137525-51-0.
