BPC-157 vs TB-500: research comparison

BPC-157 and TB-500 appear frequently in peptide research catalogs as distinct chemical listings. This page compares them as research identities — sequence, CAS, and typical laboratory framing — without medical claims or human dosing language. Both SKUs are available in the BioLabs Research catalog for laboratory use only.

BPC-157 is a pentadecapeptide derived from a gastric juice protein fragment and is commonly referenced under CAS 137525-51-0. TB-500 is a catalog name for a thymosin-β4 fragment listing (often the Ac-LKKTETQ motif) with CAS 885340-09-2 on this site’s identity map. Fragment listings are not interchangeable with intact thymosin-β4; always read the sequence printed for the lot.

BPC-157

On BioLabs Research product pages, BPC-157 is presented with CAS 137525-51-0, molecular formula C62H98N16O22, approximate molecular weight ~1419.5 Da, and sequence GEPPPGKPADDAGLV. Those fields describe the named peptide identity used in registry databases; they are not lot purity results. Lot-level HPLC/MS data belong on a certificate of analysis when issued.

In laboratory literature, BPC-157 is discussed as a stable pentadecapeptide tool compound in cell and animal model research. Catalog strengths on this site are listed as research vial options (see the live product page). For side-by-side identity checks against other SKUs, use the compound comparison tool.

Further identity notes: What is BPC-157 (research overview) and the product page BPC-157.

TB-500

TB-500 on this catalog is a fragment listing associated with CAS 885340-09-2 and the Ac-LKKTETQ sequence motif commonly marketed under the TB-500 name. Public CAS records for intact thymosin-β4 versus short fragments are not the same chemical entity. Identity for purchasing decisions should follow the sequence and any salt form stated on the vial label and COA.

Research discussions often place TB-500-type fragments in cytoskeletal and actin-binding pathway models. That is a mechanistic research context, not a use instruction. See TB-500 vs thymosin-β4 identity and TB-500 product.

Key differences

  • Different primary sequences (pentadecapeptide vs Ac-LKKTETQ fragment motif).
  • Different CAS registry numbers on the site identity map.
  • TB-500 naming can be confused with intact thymosin-β4 — verify fragment length.
  • Both are catalogued separately; a blend SKU also exists for dual-listing research.
  • Purity is lot-specific and is not implied by CAS alone.

Which for which research context

Choose BPC-157 when the protocol specifies the GEPPPGKPADDAGLV identity or cites CAS 137525-51-0.

Choose TB-500 when the protocol calls for the Ac-LKKTETQ fragment listing (CAS 885340-09-2 on this map) rather than intact thymosin-β4.

Consider the blend only if the study design explicitly requires both identities in one vial presentation — see BPC-157 / TB-500 Blend.
