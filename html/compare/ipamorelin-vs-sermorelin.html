Ipamorelin vs Sermorelin: research comparison

Ipamorelin and sermorelin are different classes of research peptides often mentioned together in growth-hormone axis literature. Ipamorelin is a selective ghrelin/GHS-R agonist pentapeptide; sermorelin is a GHRH (1–29) amide analogue. This page compares registry identities only.

Sermorelin is not a BioLabs Research catalog SKU. Ipamorelin appears on-site as part of the Tesamorelin / Ipamorelin blend listing — not as a guaranteed standalone vial. Confirm the live product page for what is actually sold.

Ipamorelin

Ipamorelin (CAS 170851-70-4) is a short peptide research tool with sequence motif Aib-His-D-2Nal-D-Phe-Lys-NH2 in public descriptions. Molecular weight is far lower than GHRH analogues. On this site, see the blend identity article and product: Tesamorelin / Ipamorelin identity, blend product.

Sermorelin

Sermorelin (CAS 86168-78-7) corresponds to GHRH (1–29) amide in public chemistry records (large peptide, multi-kilodalton range). It is not interchangeable with ipamorelin, CJC constructs, or tesamorelin. Because it is not catalogued here, use PubChem and your supplier COA for identity confirmation.

Do not assume that a GHRH analogue blend on the shelf contains sermorelin unless the COA sequence says so.

Key differences

  • Orders-of-magnitude difference in molecular weight.
  • Different receptor-class framing (GHS-R mimetic vs GHRH fragment).
  • Distinct CAS numbers; not salt forms of one another.
  • Only ipamorelin-related material appears on-catalog (blend).

Which for which research context

Ipamorelin-focused work — confirm whether you need the standalone identity or the tesamorelin/ipamorelin blend actually offered.

Sermorelin-focused work — source from a supplier that prints CAS 86168-78-7 / GHRH(1–29) on the COA; not available as a BioLabs SKU.
