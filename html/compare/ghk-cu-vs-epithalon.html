What differs at the molecular level

GHK-Cu (CAS 89030-95-5) is a glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine copper complex used as a research peptide tool. Catalog form is lyophilized; listed strength 100 mg. Confirm copper content and peptide identity on lot paperwork.

Epithalon (CAS 307297-39-8) is a synthetic tetrapeptide (often Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly in literature). Catalog strengths include 10 mg and 50 mg lyophilized vials.

Literature clusters differ: copper-peptide matrix biology versus tetrapeptide aging-model papers are not interchangeable evidence bases. Related: GHK-Cu vs BPC-157, GHK-Cu blog, epithalon blog.

Which one for your protocol?

GHK-Cu when the protocol cites CAS 89030-95-5 or copper-tripeptide complex identity.

Epithalon when the protocol cites CAS 307297-39-8 or the Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly tetrapeptide identity.

If a paper uses a trade synonym, resolve to CAS before requesting a lot.
