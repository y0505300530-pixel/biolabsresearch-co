CJC-1295 With DAC and Without DAC Are Different Molecules

August 31, 2026·BioLabs Research·8 min read

CJC-1295 with DAC vs. Modified GRF (1-29): the drug affinity complex extends hal

“CJC-1295” is used for two backbones. One carries a C-terminal Lys with a maleimidopropionyl (DAC) handle. The other is tetrasubstituted GRF(1–29) amide without that handle (often sold as “CJC-1295 no DAC” or modified GRF 1-29). They are different formulae and different masses. This site does not list either as a catalog SKU.

RUO. Laboratory reagent listing. Not for human or veterinary use. Not a medicine, food, cosmetic, or supplement. Lot HPLC, MS, and net peptide content, when issued, live on the lot COA — we do not invent a percentage on this page.

A user-facing spec that prints CAS 863288-34-0 with DAC is the pairing FDA’s compounding committee flagged as unreliable.2 Believe formula and observed mass of the maleimide, not the nickname. We will not invent HPLC% for a molecule we do not list.

The GHRH-class listing actually on this catalog is Tesamorelin / Ipamorelin — a different analogue, with its own two CAS numbers.
