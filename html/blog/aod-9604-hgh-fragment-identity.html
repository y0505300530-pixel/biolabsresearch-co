AOD-9604 vs hGH 177–191: Match the Sequence on the Vial

August 31, 2026·BioLabs Research Scientific Editorial·8 min read
BioLabs Research Scientific Editorial Catalog identity notes

AOD-9604 (hGH 177-191): the lipolytic fragment of human growth hormone, shown wi

Catalog names in this class get reused until they stop meaning anything. “AOD-9604,” “hGH 177–191,” “hGH 176–191,” and “fragment 176–191” are not automatically the same reagent. If the vial, the COA, and the purchase order disagree on the N-terminus or the disulfide, the experiment is not the listing you thought you bought.

This article is an identity note for laboratory sourcing. It does not discuss human use, body-weight protocols, or sports applications. The code name AOD-9604 comes from a discontinued medicinal-product development program. A catalog line with that name is still a laboratory reagent, not a licensed medicine.

The published listing

AOD-9604 is described in the chemical literature as a 16-residue analogue of the C-terminal region of human growth hormone, with an extra N-terminal tyrosine relative to the native 177–191 stretch, and an intramolecular disulfide between the two cysteines.1

FieldPublic listing (literature / registry)
Common nameAOD-9604 (Tyr-hGH 177–191)
CAS221231-10-3

Those numbers belong on the label as a claim about which molecule is being sold. They are not a purity. They are not a lot. Salt (TFA vs acetate) and net peptide content still have to come from the COA, as in the HPLC note.

Where catalogs mix names

Native hGH 177–191 begins with phenylalanine, not tyrosine. AOD-9604 is the tyrosine-extended / Tyr-substituted analogue used to stabilize that C-terminal fragment in published synthetic work.1 A vendor who prints “hGH 176–191” or “fragment 176–191” on a vial labeled AOD-9604 has not told you which N-terminus you have.

Two practical checks:

  • Sequence line on the COA. It should match YLRIVQCRSVEGSCGF (or the fully written Tyr-Leu-Arg-…) if the PO said AOD-9604. A Phe start is a different listing.
  • Intact mass. Tyr vs Phe is a 16 Da class difference at the N-terminus, before you even argue about disulfide or salt. If observed mass is not argued against a stated theoretical mass, you do not have identity.

CAS 221231-10-3 is the registry number used for this analogue in common chemical indexes. Older papers sometimes print other registry numbers for related fragments. If a COA’s CAS and the sequence line disagree, believe the sequence and the mass, then send the lot back.

What the name is not

AOD-9604 is not intact growth hormone. It is not a GH-receptor ligand in the sense of 191-residue somatropin. It is not interchangeable with a “176–191” bottle from a different shop. The historical development code is not an approval, an MHRA licence, or an FDA finding. We will not recap clinical endpoints here; those papers are easy to find and they are not a reason to buy a reagent.

What we put on the catalog card

The AOD-9604 catalog page lists the fragment as a laboratory reagent, with public identifiers and “lot COA on request.” We do not invent an HPLC area% on that page. If you need quantitative work, ask for the lot’s HPLC method, observed mass, and net peptide content — the same three numbers as any other lyophilized peptide.
