## Method

- Domains found via live web search (2026), then spot-checked with page fetch where possible.  
- **No invented domains, blogs, or contact emails.** Outreach angles describe *what to ask*, not fabricated addresses.  
- Mix covers: peptide RUO directories, science media/newsletters, protocol/open-science, lab marketplaces, associations, startup/trade directories, journalist-query platforms, and RUO-safe institutional adjacency.  
- Each CSV row: `domain | URL | type | why relevant | outreach angle | RUO risk | priority`

## Hard bans (never pursue)

- PBN farms / fake DR sellers / paid link networks  
- Adult sites  
- Weight-loss clinics, TRT clinics, consumer injectables marketing  
- “Buy peptides online” spam blogs and coupon farms  
- Anything that frames products as drugs, treatments, or human consumption  
- Content that would risk Google **Unapproved Substances** / domain actions  

## RUO risk key

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| low | Institutional/lab framing; directories, science media, procurement |
| med | Peptide-consumer-adjacent directories or mixed clinic/RUO inventories — stay strictly RUO in copy |
| high | Forums (Reddit etc.) — organic only; spam = ban + brand risk |
